How to measure
  • Bare skin, standing tall, shoulders relaxed, eyes straight ahead.
  • Tape level all the way around — check in a mirror.
  • Snug, not denting: touching skin all round without pressing in. Don’t suck in or flare your neck.
  • A spring‑tension tape (MyoTape, Gulick) gives the same tension every time.
  • Measure twice. If they differ by more than ¼ in (0.5 cm), take a third and average.
  • Same conditions every week: same day, morning, after the bathroom, before food, drink or exercise.
